About

Satya Narayana Nadella is an Indian-American business executive and the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Microsoft. Born on 19 August 1967 in Hyderabad, India, he completed his engineering degree in India before moving to the United States for higher studies. He joined Microsoft in 1992 and steadily rose through the company, leading major divisions such as Microsoft Business Solutions, Online Services and the Server and Tools business, where he helped build Azure into one of the world's leading cloud platforms.

In February 2014 he became Microsoft's third CEO, succeeding Steve Ballmer. Under his leadership the company shifted towards a cloud-first and AI-first strategy, embraced open source, and adopted a culture of empathy and continuous learning, which he describes in his book Hit Refresh. Microsoft's market value has grown several times over during his tenure. He is also known for his love of cricket, poetry and philanthropy.

Learn more on his Wikipedia page or the Microsoft profile.

Major Contributions

  1. Microsoft Azure

    Led the Server and Tools business and scaled Azure into a global cloud computing platform.

  2. Cloud-First Transformation

    Moved Microsoft from a Windows-centric company to a cloud and subscription-based services company, including Microsoft 365.

  3. Embracing Open Source

    Brought Linux and open source to Azure and led the acquisitions of LinkedIn (2016) and GitHub (2018).

  4. AI and Copilot

    Drove Microsoft's AI strategy, including its partnership with OpenAI and the integration of Copilot across products.

  5. Growth Mindset Culture

    Rebuilt company culture around empathy, collaboration and learning, as described in Hit Refresh.

  6. Accessibility and Inclusion

    Championed accessible technology and the Microsoft Adaptive Controller for gamers with disabilities.
